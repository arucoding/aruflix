## ❓ FAQ

**Why is the first play a bit slow?**
The server sometimes "sleeps" when nobody has used it for a while. Wait a few seconds, or tap **Retry**.

**Can I adjust the subtitles?**
Indonesian subtitles turn on automatically and are sized for comfort. There are no extra settings — kept simple on purpose.

**Is it safe?**
The app asks for no personal data at all. No accounts, no ads.

**Is it on the Play Store?**
Not yet — download it straight from here.
